By **alphabetic order**:

- **CF**: Capacity Factor, ratio of the actual energy production over a given period to the maximum possible production if the asset operated at nominal capacity throughout the period. N.B. When applied to Renewable sources, it provides a direct measure of the sun/wind "availability".

- **CY**: Climatic Year, a scenario of climatic and weather conditions for a given target year (cf. Boutheina OUESLATI's presentation on Thursday morning).

- **Dual variable**: output of a linear optimisation problem, indicating how "costly" it is to satisfy a given 
constraint. In the case of supply-demand equilibrium constraint (at a given time-slot t), the interpretation is the 
following: how costly would it be to satisfy a supplementary unit of demand at this time? 

- **ENS**: Energy Not Served, i.e. the total production of the (fictive) failure unit - over the UC period considered.

- **ERAA**: European Resource Adequacy Assessment.

- **PV**: PhotoVoltaic.

- **VRE**: Variable Renewable Energy (sources).

- **RES** : Renewable Energy Sources - including hydro., wind, PV solar.

- **Residual demand**: demand - sum of fatal (non-controllable) production (e.g., from RES).

- **TY**: Target Year, the name used in ERAA data (and study) for years studied in the future.

- **UC**: Unit Commitment, the standard name of the supply-demand equilibrium optimization problem (cf. Cécile ROTTNER's presentation on Monday morning).
